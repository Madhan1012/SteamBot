I analyzed customer transaction logs over the past 60 days and aggregated total spend across their full history. Here are the top 5 high-value accounts meeting the recency threshold:
expand_more Generated SQL 
SELECT 
  c.customer_id, 
  c.full_name, 
  c.acquisition_channel, 
  COUNT(o.order_id) AS total_orders, 
  SUM(o.total_amount) AS ltv_amount, 
  MAX(o.order_date) AS last_purchase_date 
FROM customers c 
JOIN orders o ON c.customer_id = o.customer_id 
WHERE o.order_date >= NOW() - INTERVAL '60 days' 
GROUP BY c.customer_id, c.full_name, c.acquisition_channel 
ORDER BY ltv_amount DESC 
LIMIT 5;
